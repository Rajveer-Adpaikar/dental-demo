# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective dental patients browsing the site on any device, often on mobile. They need to know the clinic is modern and trustworthy, then act: call, request a consultation, or book an appointment — ideally at any hour without waiting for office hours. The clinic owner demonstrates the site live to clients (the practice owners), so the demo must feel like a real working clinic website, not a mockup.

## Product Purpose

A high-end single-page marketing site for a dental clinic. Success = a visitor can learn about services, feel confident in the practice, and start booking an appointment themselves at any hour. The booking request lands where the practice can see and confirm it — without the site owner ever sharing Google credentials.

## Brand Personality

warm, caring, professional — friendly neighborhood dentistry with premium build quality. Trustworthy and reassuring first, impressive second.

## Anti-references

- Cold clinical "med-tech" imagery (sterile blues, gloves, machinery). People, not instruments.
- Generic AI-looking landing pages: warm-neutral cream backgrounds, tiny uppercase kickers above every section, numbered-section eyebrows, gradient text, identical card grids.
- Kiosk/enterprise SaaS vibes. This is a place you'd bring your teeth, not a dashboard.
- Anything that shouts or pressures — urgency for emergencies is legitimate, but the overall tone is calm.

## Design Principles

1. **Human first, clinical second.** Show faces and warmth; dentistry appears only as a means to confident smiles.
2. **Calm confidence.** Spacious, uncluttered, generous whitespace. No gimmicks that strain attention.
3. **Every element earns its place.** Motion and effects serve legibility and emotion, never decoration.
4. **Trust through clarity.** 24/7 booking, emergency line, and pricing/consultation info are explicit, not buried.
5. **Works live, always.** As a demo shown to clients, every button must respond; dead UI is a broken promise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast baseline; body copy ≥4.5:1.
- Reduced-motion support for all reveal/entry animations.
- Keyboard-operable menus, modal, and form; visible focus states.
- Form labels and error messages announced clearly, not color-only.
